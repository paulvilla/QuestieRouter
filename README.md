# QuestieRouter: level through every zone without ever getting lost

**QuestieRouter turns Questie's quest database into a full levelling guide.** It tells you what to pick up, where to do it and when to turn it in, in the same order an experienced player would. On top of that you get a navigation arrow, an animated route on the map, flights and boats worked out for you, dungeon guides, and an interface that changes with the zone and the time of day.

**75 zone guides · 42 dungeon guides · 12 profession guides · class guides for all 9 classes · Darkmoon Faire event guide · 3,860 quests · 97 arrow styles · 73 flight masters**

Requires **Questie** · Made for **WoW Forever** · Available in **English, Spanish, French, German and Russian**

---

## New in 0.10.0: the guide browser

Press **Esc** and click **QuestieRouter** (or type `/qr menu`) to open the new guide browser.

- **Sections:** Zones, Dungeons, Professions, Class, Events, Speedrun (coming soon) and Other, plus Options.
- **Cards** with each zone's artwork, and on the right the guide itself: every quest with its status, a **Map** button and **Load guide**.
- **Map:** opens the world map where the next quest to pick up is and marks it with a waypoint. Every quest in the list has its own Map button.
- **Descriptions and narration** of zones and dungeons from **Spoken** (optional), played through Spoken's own player so it keeps going while you play.
- **Options** now live inside the browser, with an index that jumps to each section.

## Walking routes over the terrain

The arrow and the map line follow the **real way on foot**: around mountains, through gates and cave mouths, along roads, never swimming across open sea. Choose the style that suits you:

- **Fastest:** the shortest way, straight across the land.
- **Balanced:** prefers roads, but cuts across when the road makes a long detour.
- **Roads only:** follows roads and paths whenever there is one.

The walking maps are loaded on demand, only for the continent you are on (see *Licences* below).

## Dungeons: bosses and loot

Every dungeon has its loading-screen artwork and a **Bosses and loot** tab: every boss, what it drops and the drop chance, with a filter by class and item type (cloth, leather, mail, plate, weapons, recipes...). Bosses with a Spoken narration can be listened to from the list.

## Profession guides

Guides from **1 to 300 for all 12 professions**, made for WoW Forever: trainers, recipes to learn, materials to buy or gather, where each material drops and a shopping list of what you still need. The **Recipes** tab lists every recipe of the profession with its skill levels, materials and where to learn it.

## Class guides and skills

- **Class quest chains for all 9 classes**, named after what you get (Summon Voidwalker, Whirlwind Weapon, Aquatic Form...), with every reward listed and the class banner.
- **Skills:** everything your class trainer teaches by level, what you can learn right now, and the abilities that come from quests (demons, stances, forms) with a button that takes you to their quest.
- A **notice when you level up** and there are new skills to learn at your trainer.

## Other guides

**Cozy Sleeping Bag** and **Library Books**, with every quest and its rewards.

## A guide for every zone

![Zone guides](https://i.imgur.com/4FdocSV.png)

- One guide per zone and faction, for both Alliance and Horde, including **Zephras Isle**, the **Darkspear Islands (PvP)**, **Mount Hyjal**, **Riverglades** and **Shen'dralas**.
- Only quests that exist on WoW Forever: nothing from other expansions and nothing above level 60.
- Each guide uses the level range shown on the zone's map, so you always know whether a zone suits you.
- Quests are sorted by level and distance, and every prerequisite is respected.
- Each zone starts with its entry quest.
- City quests are placed in the zone where you actually do them.
- The guide that best fits your level is marked as recommended.

## Pick up · Do · Turn in

![Planner](https://i.imgur.com/wKixRgL.png)

Forget doing one quest at a time. QuestieRouter plans your session the way an experienced player does:

1. **Hub:** pick up and turn in everything nearby.
2. **Field:** head to the objective area, or straight to the densest group of enemies.
3. **Turn in:** finish the rest. Anything newly unlocked takes you back to the hub.
4. **Next hub:** move on, without bouncing back and forth between two nearby quests.

**In capital cities**, every completed quest you can turn in there comes first, starting with the nearest NPC. Then the guide picks up where it left off.

## Dungeon guides

![Dungeon guides](https://i.imgur.com/RcLV06E.png)

42 dungeon guides, from The Deadmines to Scholomance. QuestieRouter sends you around the world to collect the dungeon's quests, then routes you to the entrance. Steps done inside the dungeon are clearly marked, and once you're out it takes you to turn everything in.

## Event guides

![Event guides](https://i.imgur.com/SixqCfS.jpeg)

A new **Event guides** category, between the profession and speedrun guides. The **Darkmoon Faire** is the first one; the rest of the world events are already listed under **Coming soon** and will arrive as they reach the game.

- **Always in the right place:** the faire moves between **Elwynn Forest** and **Mulgore** every month. QuestieRouter knows whether it's up and where, and every faire NPC points to that zone.
- **Not up?** The menu and the banner tell you the date and place of the next faire.
- **Tickets for every prize:** one route that takes you through every ticket turn-in and then buys the prizes from cheapest to most expensive, up to the Orb and the Amulet of the Darkmoon. The banner shows your tickets and the next prize.
- **One section per NPC:** Chronos (leatherworking), Kerri Hicks (blacksmithing), Rinling (engineering) and Yebb Neblegear (farmed items), plus Sayge's fortune, the card decks and the pets (Jubjub and Flik's frogs).
- Crafted turn-ins are only suggested when you already carry the items; farmed ones take you to the creatures that drop them.
- **Real levels:** the game lists every faire quest as level 60, so QuestieRouter shows the level each one really requires and the level range of each section.
- Prize redemptions use a **coin icon**, since they are just a purchase.

## Quest chains and quest list

![Quest chains](https://i.imgur.com/hlMVhV6.png)

The **Quests** tab lists every quest in the zone and shows exactly where you stand on each one: available, in progress, ready to turn in or locked.

- **What's missing:** if a quest isn't available yet, the list shows the earlier quest you still need and its zone.
- **The whole chain at a glance:** right-click any quest to see its chain, numbered in the order you do it. Each quest shows its status on the right: done, in progress, ready to turn in, available, the level you still need, or locked, with the reason when it isn't an earlier quest.
- **One click to start:** click any quest in the chain to get a route to whoever gives it. If you can't pick it up yet, you get a warning instead of a wasted trip.
- **Only what's yours:** quests for another race or class never show up as the previous quest.
- Skip quests and bring them back whenever you like.
- **Elite** and **dungeon** quests are marked with icons in the list, in the steps and in the chain.

## Auto accept and turn in

![Automation](https://i.imgur.com/Naq56mn.png)

Talk to an NPC and QuestieRouter accepts the guide's quests and turns in the completed ones for you.

- It only accepts quests from the guide's steps, and never anything far above your level.
- When there's a reward to choose, the choice is always yours.
- Hold **Shift** to skip the automation for that NPC.
- Turn it on or off from the right-click menu or in Settings.

## Navigation arrow

![Arrow](https://i.imgur.com/SmNGVcB.png)

Choose from 97 original arrow styles, in the zone's colour or any colour you like. Under the arrow you can see what's left to do and how far away it is. Once you reach the objective area, the arrow moves out of your way.

## Animated map route

![Map route](https://i.imgur.com/KW7IKlx.png)

An animated line runs from you to the objective, with the step number, on both the world map and the minimap.

- 4 line styles to choose from.
- The line keeps the same thickness at any zoom level.
- Travel routes are drawn leg by leg.
- Smooth zooming, also with OpenMap: lines and markers are only redrawn when something changes, and flight paths use fewer points.

## Targets and items

![Targets and items](https://i.imgur.com/GzYwY3T.png)

Two compact windows sit right next to the guide.

**Target window**
- Shows the current step's enemies, plus the NPC you need to pick up from or turn in to.
- Left-click to target. Each button places its own raid marker, and only living enemies are selected.
- Shows a square 2D portrait or a 3D model. Right-click to talk to the NPC or cast the ability of your choice.

**Items window**
- Lists your usable quest items, ready with one click.
- Items for the current step come first, with their count and cooldown.

Place both windows anywhere and set their button size and title position. Both are safe to use in combat and update as soon as combat ends.

## Travel and flights

![Travel](https://i.imgur.com/CV60T0K.png)

When the objective is far away, QuestieRouter finds the fastest way there using:

- **The flights you've learned.** They're checked automatically every time you open a flight master's map.
- **Boats and zeppelins**, including waiting and travel times.
- **The tram and portals** of your faction.
- **City gates**, so routes never go through walls.

While you fly, a bar at the bottom of the screen counts down your arrival based on real flight times.

## Getting started

1. Install **Questie** and **QuestieRouter**. The download includes four folders (`QuestieRouter`, `QuestieRouter_Nav`, `QuestieRouter_Nav_EK` and `QuestieRouter_Nav_Kalimdor`): copy all of them to `Interface/AddOns`.
2. Log in. A short **setup wizard** opens the first time: the walking route style, the text size and what the guide shows. You can open it again at any time with `/qr install`.
3. Press **Esc → QuestieRouter**, pick a guide and click **Load guide**, or take the recommended zone.
4. Follow the arrow.

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/qr` | Turn QuestieRouter on or off |
| `/qr guide` | Show or hide the guide window |
| `/qr menu` | Open the guide browser |
| `/qr pick` | Open the zone picker |
| `/qr options` | Graphics options |
| `/qr settings` | Panel in Settings > AddOns (changelog, support) |
| `/qr install` | Open the setup wizard again |
| `/qr news` | Show what's new in this version |
| `/qr times` | How long QuestieRouter waited for Questie at startup |
| `/qr dungeon <name>` | Route to a dungeon entrance |
| `/qr perf` | Measure for 10 seconds how long the map lines take (open the world map and zoom) |
| `/qr load_en` · `load_es` · `load_fr` · `load_de` · `load_ru` | Change the addon's language |
| `/qr load_auto` | Use your client's language |

You can also **right-click the banner** or the minimap button to open the full menu.

## Languages

The interface is fully translated into:

- **Spanish** (esES / esMX)
- **French** (frFR)
